ChessScroll ("ChessScroll," "we," "us," or "our") is an iPhone app that places the apps you choose behind a chess puzzle: solve the puzzle, earn minutes of use. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: ChessScroll has no accounts and no sign-in. Which apps you seal and how you spend your screen time stay on your device — Apple's Screen Time framework enforces that by design, and we could not read them even if we wanted to. Puzzles work fully offline. The only third party involved is RevenueCat, which manages your membership.

1. Information we handle

Your sealed apps. When you pick the apps ChessScroll should seal, your selection is handled by Apple's Family Controls and Screen Time frameworks. The selection is stored on your device as opaque tokens that identify the apps to the system but are not readable by us. It never leaves your device.

Screen-time and app-usage data. Timing and usage of your sealed apps is processed on-device by Apple's Screen Time framework inside sandboxed system extensions. Apple's architecture prevents this data from being transmitted to us or to anyone else. We receive nothing.

Chess activity. Your puzzle attempts, puzzle rating, streak, earned minutes, and settings are stored locally on your device. The puzzle library ships inside the app, so solving works fully offline. There is no account and no sign-in.

Purchase and membership data. When you subscribe, the transaction is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage membership status. RevenueCat receives a randomized app user identifier and purchase receipt information so the app knows whether your membership is active. We never see your payment details.

Diagnostics. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect if you have enabled sharing in your device settings. This data does not identify you.

2. What we do not do

  • No accounts, no email addresses, no personal profiles.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, no selling of data.
  • No reading of your browsing, messages, or activity inside sealed apps.
  • No server of ours ever receives your chess activity or screen-time data.

3. Third-party services

ChessScroll relies on two providers to function. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple (App Store, Screen Time / Family Controls) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat (membership management) — revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Data storage and retention

Your puzzle history, rating, and settings are stored on your device. If you delete the app, this local data is removed with it, and any Screen Time restrictions ChessScroll applied are lifted. Membership records held by Apple and RevenueCat are retained according to their policies.

5. Children

ChessScroll is a self-restriction tool you apply to your own device. It is not directed at children and is not a parental-control product. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Your choices

  • Change or remove your sealed apps at any time inside ChessScroll.
  • Manage or cancel your membership in your App Store account settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
